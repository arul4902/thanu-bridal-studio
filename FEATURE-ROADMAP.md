# Thanushiya Bridal Studio — features with business value

## Immediate content upgrade (requested)

- Add verified client review videos with a descriptive title, accessible playback controls, captions/transcript where available, original Instagram link, and a nearby date-enquiry CTA.
- Add a distinct Celebrity Artistry gallery. Name each person only when the source identifies them and credits the studio. Preserve photographer credits. A makeup credit must not be presented as a recommendation or testimonial.
- Use direct uploaded video files where the studio supplies originals; use supported public Instagram embeds only when the actual post is accessible and embedding is enabled. Always retain a useful original-post link. Do not promise that highlights can play outside Instagram.
- Avoid autoplay with sound. Load videos after a visitor chooses to watch, preserving mobile speed.

## Ranked feature recommendations

| Priority | Feature | Visitor experience | Owner benefit | Needed to implement well |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Find your bridal look | Choose wedding/reception/engagement and preferred finish; see relevant real looks | More useful enquiries and a distinctive consultation experience | Approved portfolio images with accurate tags |
| 2 | Save my inspiration | Shortlist looks and include their names/links in the WhatsApp enquiry | Less back-and-forth about the desired style | Stable look IDs and source links; clear device-local storage behavior |
| 3 | Review-to-enquiry journey | Watch a genuine bride’s review, view her look, enquire for a date | Connects trust with a clear next step | Verified original review video, attribution and permission |
| 4 | Tamil / English | Switch all key copy, FAQs and enquiry labels between languages | Makes the service easier for families to understand | Owner-reviewed Tamil translation |
| 5 | Private enquiry dashboard | Owner tracks new enquiries, quoted clients, confirmed dates and follow-ups | Reduces missed leads and makes booking progress visible | Authentication, persistent storage and owner workflow agreement |
| 6 | Availability and consultation slots | Request an available consultation time or wedding date | Saves scheduling effort | A calendar the owner maintains; do not imply live availability without it |
| 7 | Booking-source insights | Owner sees which portfolio categories and CTAs lead to enquiries | Helps decide which work to feature | Defined analytics events and appropriate privacy disclosure |

## Recommended owner demonstration

Show one continuous journey: choose an occasion → shortlist two real looks → watch a relevant bride’s review → send date, venue and selected looks on WhatsApp.

This demonstrates the website’s value in the studio’s own workflow. Build recommendations 1–3 first, then bilingual copy. Introduce a dashboard only if the owner will use it consistently.

## Practical limits

- WhatsApp link clicks are not confirmed bookings. Revenue and conversion claims require actual tracking and booking records.
- Do not add an AI face makeover as the first feature: simulated makeup can misrepresent the artist’s achievable results and introduces photo-handling requirements.
- Do not use a chatbot to invent package prices, availability, skin-suitability claims or booking policies. A guided enquiry flow can answer verified FAQs and collect preferences first.
- Recommendations are proposed additions, not features already implemented.
